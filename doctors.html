Readico for doctors

A simple companion for patients who take regular medication

Readico helps patients and the people who care for them stay on top of medicines and symptoms, and gives you a clear picture to work from at the appointment.

What patients get

  • Dose reminders that nudge again until a dose is marked taken or skipped.
  • Clear tracking of doses taken, skipped and missed.
  • Symptom logging, including a spoken daily check-in for people who find typing hard.
  • Simple, large screens that work with larger text sizes.

What you get: the visit summary

Before an appointment, the patient can create a summary and bring it, or share it, as a PDF. It brings together their current medications, how consistently doses have been taken, and recent symptoms, so the conversation can start from what actually happened rather than from memory.

Nothing is sent to a clinician automatically. The patient decides what to share and when.

Who it is designed for

Older adults, people who find typing or small screens difficult, and carers who help someone manage their medicines. It is designed to be simple and forgiving to use.

Privacy, in plain words

  • A patient can start without creating an account, and can later back up their account with Google so they can recover it on another phone.
  • Information is shared with a clinician only when the patient chooses to share their summary.
Readico is an organising tool. It does not diagnose, give medical advice, or replace a clinician’s judgement.
