How it works

Three simple steps. No jargon, no fuss.

1

Tell Readico about your medicines

Speak or type your medicines, the strength and when you take them. Edit anything at any time.

2

Get reminders and check in

Mark each dose with a tap or your voice. Readico remembers what you have taken, and when.

3

Share a clear summary

Bring a ready-made summary to your doctor, so the visit starts from what really happened.

A summary your doctor can use

Readico brings your medicines, their strength and how consistently you took them into a single page you can print or share.

  • Strength and timing of every medicine, including before or after food
  • What was taken, skipped, missed or not recorded
  • Symptoms you logged, with how serious they were
  • Questions you want to ask, all in one place
Readico is an organising tool. It does not diagnose, give medical advice, or replace a clinician’s judgement. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.
